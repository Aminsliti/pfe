\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This end-of-studies project focuses on the design and implementation of an integrated business process management platform, called \textbf{V-BPM Platform}. This innovative solution aims to facilitate the modeling, simulation, and management of business processes while ensuring compliance with current standards and regulations.

The platform provides a comprehensive environment enabling process designers to model in BPMN, simulate process execution, manage the company organizational chart, and collaborate effectively. The technical architecture relies on modern technologies: React.js for the frontend, Node.js with Express for the backend, and SQLite as the database management system.

This report presents the different phases of the project, from requirements specification to the implementation of various functionalities organized into three sprints, through architectural design and UML modeling.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\textbf{Keywords:} BPMN, BPM, process simulation, business process management, compliance, organizational chart, React, Node.js, SQLite, V-GRC.
