\chapter*{General Introduction}
\markboth{General Introduction}{}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{General Introduction}

In the context of digital transformation, organizations face increasing pressure to optimize their business processes while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and managing operational risks. The convergence of Business Process Management (BPM) with Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) has become essential for maintaining competitiveness and sustainability in today's dynamic business environment.

This report presents the design and implementation of a BPM platform integrated with a GRC system, developed for Vitalis Tunisia. The project addresses a critical challenge faced by many organizations: the need to replace proprietary BPM solutions with flexible, cost-effective open-source alternatives while maintaining seamless integration with existing governance frameworks.

The objective of this project was to implement an open-source BPM solution that integrates with Vitalis's existing GRC platform. Through dedicated APIs, the solution ensures data synchronization, alignment of risks with processes, and comprehensive incident reporting. The resulting platform provides Vitalis with an integrated solution for modeling workflows following BPMN standards, real-time process control, risk management during process execution, and performance indicator analysis.

The implemented solution transforms operational management into a strategic decision-making tool, built on a modern technological stack comprising a dynamic front-end, robust back-end, and powerful BPM execution engine. This approach eliminates vendor dependency, reduces licensing costs, and provides greater flexibility for future evolution.

This report is organized into six chapters that systematically present the methodology and results of the project:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Project Context and Problem Statement:} Presentation of Vitalis, project context, challenges, and objectives.
\item \textbf{State of the Art:} Theoretical foundations covering GRC, BPM, standards, and related technologies.
\item \textbf{Specification of Needs:} Identification and analysis of functional and non-functional requirements, system constraints, and use cases.
\item \textbf{Sprint 0: Authentication \& Data Management:} Project initialization, environment setup, core authentication, and data management features.
\item \textbf{Sprint 1: Organizational Chart and Simulations:} Design and implementation of organizational chart management, simulation capabilities, and BPMN modeling.
\item \textbf{Sprint 2: Compliance and V-GRC Integration:} Integration with the GRC system, advanced features implementation, testing, and validation.
\end{itemize}

Each chapter builds upon the previous one, providing a comprehensive view of the project from conceptualization through implementation and validation, demonstrating how modern open-source technologies can effectively address complex business process management challenges.
